      Crypto price and RSI alerts

      Check on demand while this local app is running. No background monitoring, email or push notifications. A rule triggers on its first matching check, then stays quiet until a valid check clears the condition. Above/below comparisons are strict; equality clears the match.

      Price quotes must be no older than 15 minutes. RSI uses 90 complete UTC daily observations and Wilder RSI(14), including the current day. A check can miss movements between checks.

      Checks call CoinGecko, never OpenAI.
