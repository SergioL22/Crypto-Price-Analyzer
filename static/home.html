What you can do

Company fundamentals

Revenue, margins, cash flow, EPS and share count for up to ten years, for any US-listed company. Every number links to the filing it came from.

Filing alerts

Follow companies and get a NEW badge on annual reports, material events and insider trades filed since your last visit.

Insider trading

A year of Form 4 filings summarised: open-market buys and sales by executives and directors, kept apart from routine grants, with pre-planned sales flagged.

Inside an ETF

Top holdings, concentration and size for SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA and VTI, and how much two funds overlap, from their N-PORT reports.

Your price data

Open a CSV from your broker or a spreadsheet for charts, moving averages, RSI, volatility, worst drop, fund comparisons, a what-if calculator and alert rules. The file never leaves your browser.

Live charts

Real-time charts from TradingView, plus a watchlist saved in your browser.

Why there are no stock prices here

Free market-data services license their prices for personal use. Showing them, or results calculated from them, on a public website needs a paid business license. Research Desk is built on what can be shared freely: SEC filings are public information. Price tools run on data you bring, in your own browser, and live charts come from TradingView's own embed.

Research only, not investment advice. No accounts, no trading. Practice mode uses clearly labelled fictional companies.
