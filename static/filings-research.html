      How this data works
      Where it comes from
      Fundamentals use XBRL data that companies file with the SEC, using the SEC's calendar-year frames to avoid double counting. Holdings come from each fund's latest public Form N-PORT report, which the SEC publishes about 60 days after each fiscal quarter.
      Calculated measures
      Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus capital expenditure. Margins divide operating or net income by revenue. Banks and some other companies do not report every measure, so some charts are empty.
      What is left out
      Short-term cash vehicles, such as reinvested securities-lending collateral, are excluded from ETF holdings and overlap. Their share is shown separately.
      Freshness
      The data is a periodic snapshot, not live. Check the linked SEC filing for the exact reported figure.
