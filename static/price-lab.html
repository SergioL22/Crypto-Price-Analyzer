Price tools on your own file

Open a CSV of daily prices, such as an export from your broker or a spreadsheet. Research Desk charts it, calculates indicators and risk measures, compares funds, runs a hypothetical investment and checks your alert rules.

Your file never leaves this browser. It is read on your device, nothing is uploaded, and it is cleared when you close the tab. Results are only as accurate as the file. Research only, not advice.

Your files

Each file needs a header row with a Date column and a Close, Adj Close or Price column. Adjusted close is used when present. Dates can be 2024-01-31 or 1/31/2024. Open up to four files to compare them.

    Hypothetical investment

    Buys at that day's price in your file, with fractional shares, no fees, taxes or dividends unless they are reflected in an adjusted price. No trades are placed.

    Alert rules

    Rules are checked against the latest row of each file whenever you open or refresh it. Rules are saved in this browser by series name, so they come back when you open an updated file with the same name.

      Calculations run in your browser on data you provide. Research Desk does not supply, store or redistribute market prices.
