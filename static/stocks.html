About prediction research

We tested ten stocks and ETFs across 2021–2025 and partial 2026. The current model did not beat an always-up baseline in any yearly average across these assets. It is not approved for stock recommendations.

Historical test accuracy is not the chance that the next prediction will be correct. Future predictions must be recorded before their outcomes and evaluated against simple baselines. No validated stock prediction is shown here.
