    Help & glossary
    Ticker
    A short code identifying a stock or fund, such as AAPL.
    ETF
    An exchange-traded fund: a basket of investments traded on an exchange.
    Candlestick
    A chart bar showing the opening, highest, lowest and closing prices for a period.
    Delayed data
    Prices shown after a time delay rather than immediately as trades occur. Check the chart's data status.
    Practice tools
    The simulator and alerts use fictional listings and generated prices. They do not monitor real stocks.
    Charts powered by TradingView. Research only. Chart data is not available to our simulator, alert engine or AI research.
