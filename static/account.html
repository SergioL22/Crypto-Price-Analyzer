Plain-language glossary

RSI describes recent momentum on a 0–100 scale. MACD compares moving averages. Bollinger Bands describe historical price variation. These are descriptive signals, not price predictions.

AI confidence is the model’s qualitative judgment about the supplied evidence. Direction accuracy is the fraction of recorded five-session up/down forecasts that matched completed outcomes. Brier score measures probability error; smaller is better. Calibration compares predicted probabilities with observed frequencies.

About predictions

The current research model has not established a reliable advantage over simple baselines. Prospective predictions are recorded separately and scored only after their five-session targets finish. No validated accuracy or buy/sell prediction is shown here.
